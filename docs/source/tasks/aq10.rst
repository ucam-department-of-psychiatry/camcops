Autism Spectrum Quotient – 10 items (AQ-10) (Adult)
---------------------------------------------------

.. include:: include_permission_contextual.rst

A 10-item self-administered "red flag" instrument to help frontline health
professions decide whether to make a referral for a full diagnostic
assessment for an autism spectrum condition (ASC) in adults.

The 10 questions are taken from :ref:`Autism Spectrum Quotient (AQ) (Adult)
<aq>` and they are scored in the same way.

If the individual scores 6 or above, consider referring them for a specialist
diagnostic assessment.
